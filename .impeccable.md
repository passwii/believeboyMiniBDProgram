## Design Context

### Users

The admin console is used primarily by the owner on a desktop, with regular
mobile use when away from the desk. Staff use is planned but not yet active.
The core jobs are uploading a self-contained HTML proposal, creating a public
or private share, copying a link, and revoking access quickly. Customers do
not use the admin console; they only open the shared H5 viewer or the WeChat
mini program.

### Brand Personality

Restrained, trustworthy, editorial. The interface should feel like the private
studio tool behind a premium proposal service: calm, exact, and discreet
rather than corporate or technical for its own sake.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use a warm paper-like light theme with ink-black type, restrained sage or
vermillion accents, and strong editorial typography. Desktop layouts should
have high information density and clear scan paths. Mobile layouts must retain
every important action rather than hiding functionality. Avoid blue-purple
SaaS gradients, generic card grids, glassmorphism, and decorative dashboards.

### Design Principles

1. Make the next operational action obvious at every viewport size.
2. Treat sensitive content with quiet confidence: no flashy visuals, no
   accidental exposure, no ambiguous status.
3. Use editorial hierarchy and asymmetric composition instead of uniform cards.
4. Keep destructive actions explicit and reversible where possible.
5. Optimize desktop efficiency while preserving complete mobile workflows.
